[Step 6] Self-Assembled Monolayer (SAM) & Bio-Receptor Functionalization
  Instructions:
    * Thiolation: Submerge chip in 10 mM 11-Mercaptoundecanoic acid (11-MUA) in anhydrous ethanol for 18 hours in darkness to form a dense self-assembled monolayer (-COOH terminated).

    * Carboxyl Activation: Treat with freshly prepared 0.4 M EDC (1-ethyl-3-(3-dimethylaminopropyl)carbodiimide) and 0.1 M NHS (N-hydroxysuccinimide) in 10 mM MES buffer (pH 5.5) for 25 minutes.

    * Surface Blocking: Quench unreacted NHS esters with 1.0 M Ethanolamine-HCl (pH 8.5) for 10 minutes followed by 1% Bovine Serum Albumin (BSA) in PBS to eliminate non-specific binding.
